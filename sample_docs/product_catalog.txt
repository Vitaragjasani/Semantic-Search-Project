Product Catalog Excerpt - Replacement Parts

Part number ACME-4471-X is the replacement cooling fan assembly for the Model 200 series units. It is compatible with units manufactured after 2022 and includes a 2-year limited warranty.

Part number ACME-3310-B is the replacement power supply unit for the Model 100 series. It is not compatible with Model 200 series units.

For installation instructions, contact technical support and reference the part number above.
