# Refund and Exchange Policy

## Refunds

Refunds are processed within 10 business days of us receiving the returned item. The refund is issued to the original payment method. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or color are processed within 5 business days. Exchanged items ship free of charge within the continental US. International exchanges may incur additional shipping fees.

## Eligibility

Items must be returned within 30 days of delivery, unused, and in their original packaging. Final sale items are not eligible for refund or exchange.
